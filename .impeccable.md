## Design Context

### Users
Italian university students, primarily 19–26 years old. Using the app evenings and late nights to process lecture material, prepare for exams, and manage academic schedules. Context: low-light environment, long study sessions, high cognitive load from academic content.

### Brand Personality
Focused, intelligent, quietly powerful. Three words: **precise, ambient, academic**.
Not playful or casual. Not corporate or sterile. Like a well-designed research terminal — serious but not intimidating.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark mode only (appropriate for the audience's nocturnal study sessions). Emerald green as primary accent — distinctive enough but avoids the default cyan/purple AI palette. The interface should feel like a tool used by someone who takes their studies seriously. Think: quality notebook, not a consumer app.

Anti-references: avoid generic SaaS dashboard feel, avoid excessive gradients, avoid anything that screams "built with AI".

### Design Principles
1. **Clarity over decoration** — information density is an asset, decoration a liability
2. **Consistent spatial rhythm** — same padding, same spacing scale throughout
3. **Subdued until active** — elements reveal their interactivity on focus/hover, not by default
4. **Accessible at all hours** — dark mode with proper contrast, no pure black, readable at 2am
5. **Typography does the heavy lifting** — font weight and size create hierarchy, not color or borders
